Santosh Medical College, Ghaziabad

Deemed medical university with modern academic and clinical facilities

Medical College Uttar Pradesh Deemed University

College Overview

Santosh Medical College, Ghaziabad is a private deemed medical university offering undergraduate and postgraduate medical education. The institution is known for its modern infrastructure, well-equipped teaching hospital, and experienced faculty.

Medical Courses Offered

  • MBBS (Bachelor of Medicine & Surgery)
  • MD – General Medicine
  • MD – Pediatrics
  • MS – General Surgery
  • MS – Orthopaedics
  • Other PG Medical Courses

Admission Process

Admissions are granted through NEET-UG for MBBS and NEET-PG for postgraduate courses via centralized counselling.

Key Highlights

  • Recognized by National Medical Commission (NMC)
  • Modern hospital and diagnostic facilities
  • Strong clinical exposure
  • Located in NCR region for better opportunities
