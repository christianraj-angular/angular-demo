One of India’s oldest and most respected government medical colleges

Medical College Government College Madhya Pradesh

College Overview

Mahatma Gandhi Memorial (MGM) Medical College, Indore is a prestigious government medical institution affiliated with Devi Ahilya Vishwavidyalaya. The college is known for academic excellence, research, and extensive clinical exposure through its associated hospitals.

Medical Courses Offered

  • MBBS (Undergraduate Program)
  • MD – General Medicine
  • MD – Pediatrics
  • MS – General Surgery
  • MS – Orthopaedics
  • Other PG & Super-Speciality Programs

Admission Process

Admissions to MGM Medical College Indore are conducted based on NEET-UG and NEET-PG scores. Centralized counselling through MCC / state authorities is mandatory.

Key Highlights

  • One of the oldest government medical colleges in India
  • High patient inflow for clinical training
  • Renowned faculty and alumni
  • Strong emphasis on research and academics
