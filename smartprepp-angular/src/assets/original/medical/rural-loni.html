College Overview

Rural Medical College, Loni is a renowned medical institution established by the Pravara Institute of Medical Sciences. The college focuses on providing quality medical education while addressing the healthcare needs of rural communities through extensive clinical exposure.

Medical Courses Offered

  • MBBS (Bachelor of Medicine & Surgery)
  • MD – General Medicine
  • MD – Pediatrics
  • MS – General Surgery
  • MS – Orthopaedics
  • Postgraduate & Super-Speciality Programs

Admission Process

Admissions to Rural Medical College, Loni are conducted based on NEET-UG and NEET-PG scores. Eligible candidates must participate in centralized counselling.

Key Highlights

  • Strong focus on rural healthcare services
  • Large teaching hospital with diverse patient exposure
  • Experienced faculty and research facilities
  • Community-oriented medical education model
