Medical College Maharashtra Deemed University

College Overview

Krishna Institute of Medical Sciences (KIMS), Karad is a reputed deemed university in Maharashtra offering quality medical education with a strong focus on clinical exposure, research and community healthcare.

Medical Courses Offered

  • MBBS (Undergraduate Program)
  • MD – General Medicine
  • MD – Pediatrics
  • MS – General Surgery
  • MS – Orthopaedics
  • Allied Health & Super-Speciality Programs

Admission Process

Admissions to KIMS Karad are based on NEET-UG and NEET-PG examinations followed by centralized counselling.

Key Highlights

  • Deemed university status
  • Well-equipped teaching hospital
  • Strong academic and clinical exposure
  • Focus on research and rural healthcare
