MGM Medical College & Research Institute, Puducherry

One of the leading private medical colleges in South India

College Overview

Mahatma Gandhi Medical College & Research Institute (MGMCRI) is a reputed private medical college located in Puducherry. The college is affiliated with Sri Balaji Vidyapeeth (Deemed University) and is known for excellent clinical exposure, modern infrastructure and experienced faculty.

Medical Courses Offered

  • MBBS
  • MD – General Medicine
  • MS – General Surgery
  • Pediatrics, Orthopaedics, OBG, Anaesthesiology and more

Admission Process

Admissions to MGM Medical College are based on NEET UG and NEET PG scores. Eligible students participate in centralized counselling conducted by MCC or state authorities.

Key Highlights

  • Teaching hospital with high patient inflow
  • Advanced laboratories and modern campus
  • Strong academic and clinical training
  • Opportunities for research and specialization
