Cookies Policy

How Smart Prepp uses cookies to enhance your experience

1. Introduction

This Cookies Policy explains how Smart Prepp uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve user experience, analyze website performance, and provide personalized services. By continuing to use our website, you agree to our use of cookies.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognize your device, remember your preferences, and enhance your browsing experience. Cookies do not give us access to your personal device or files.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for website functionality such as navigation and security.
  • Analytics Cookies: Helps us understand how visitors use our site (Google Analytics, etc.).
  • Preference Cookies: Saves your preferences like theme, language, or form selections.
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to display personalized ads and measure marketing performance.

4. Why We Use Cookies

  • To improve website efficiency and loading speed.
  • To remember your preferences and settings.
  • To monitor website traffic and usage patterns.
  • To personalize your experience based on your interactions.

5. Third-Party Cookies

Smart Prepp may allow trusted third parties to place cookies on your device for analytics, advertising, and performance optimization. These third-party providers follow strict privacy and data protection rules.

6. Managing & Disabling Cookies

You have full control over cookie settings. You can:

  • Disable cookies directly from your browser settings.
  • Clear existing cookies anytime.
  • Block third-party cookies for enhanced privacy.

Note: Disabling cookies may limit certain website features.

7. Updates to This Cookies Policy

Smart Prepp may update this policy from time to time to reflect new regulations or improvements in our services. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
