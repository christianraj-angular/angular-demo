College Overview

SRM Institute of Science & Technology (SRMIST) is a leading private deemed university headquartered in Kattankulathur, Tamil Nadu. It offers world-class education with strong industry partnerships and international exposure.

Popular Engineering Programs

  • B.Tech – CSE, AI, Data Science, Cyber Security
  • B.Tech – ECE, Mechanical, Civil
  • M.Tech & interdisciplinary programs
  • Research-driven Ph.D programs

Admission Process

Admissions to B.Tech programs are based on SRMJEEE. Candidates apply online and participate in counselling rounds.

Highlights

  • Multiple campuses across India
  • Strong placement record with top MNCs
  • Modern hostels & research labs
  • International exchange programs
