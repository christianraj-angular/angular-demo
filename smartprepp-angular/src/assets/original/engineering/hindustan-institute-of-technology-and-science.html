Engineering College Deemed University Chennai

College Overview

Hindustan Institute of Technology & Science (HITS), located in Chennai, is a reputed deemed-to-be university established in 1985. It is particularly known for engineering, aerospace, aviation, and applied science programs.

Popular Engineering Programs

  • B.Tech – Computer Science & Engineering
  • B.Tech – Artificial Intelligence & Data Science
  • B.Tech – Aerospace Engineering
  • B.Tech – Mechanical Engineering
  • B.Tech – Electronics & Communication Engineering

Admission Process

Admissions are conducted through HITS Engineering Entrance Examination and merit-based selection depending on the program.

Highlights

  • Strong focus on aviation & aerospace studies
  • NAAC A accredited institution
  • Industry-oriented and practical learning
  • Good placement support
