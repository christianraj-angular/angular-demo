Engineering College Karnataka Deemed University

College Overview

Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE) is a renowned private deemed university located in Manipal, Karnataka. Established in 1953, MAHE is known for its academic quality, global collaborations, and industry-focused education.

Popular Engineering Programs

  • B.Tech – Computer Science & Engineering
  • B.Tech – AI & Machine Learning
  • B.Tech – Data Science
  • B.Tech – Electronics & Communication
  • B.Tech – Mechanical & Civil Engineering
  • M.Tech & Research Programs

Admission Process

Admissions to B.Tech programs are through MET (Manipal Entrance Test). Qualified students are invited for counselling and seat allotment.

Highlights

  • International campuses & partnerships
  • Strong alumni network worldwide
  • Excellent placement opportunities
  • Modern infrastructure & hostels
