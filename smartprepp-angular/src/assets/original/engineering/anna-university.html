Anna University, Chennai

One of India’s leading public technical universities

Engineering College Tamil Nadu Public University

College Overview

Anna University is a prestigious public state university located in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. Established in 1978, it offers technical education and affiliating support to hundreds of engineering colleges across the state.

Popular Engineering Programs

  • B.E – Computer Science & Engineering
  • B.E – Electronics & Communication Engineering
  • B.E – Electrical & Electronics Engineering
  • B.E – Mechanical & Civil Engineering
  • M.E / M.Tech Programs

Admission Process

Admissions are conducted through TNEA Counselling based on Class 12 marks. There is no entrance exam for undergraduate engineering admissions.
