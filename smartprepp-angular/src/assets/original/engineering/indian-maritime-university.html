College Overview

Indian Maritime University (IMU) is a central university under the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways, Government of India. IMU focuses exclusively on maritime education and offers specialized engineering and nautical programs across multiple campuses.

Popular Engineering Programs

  • B.Tech – Marine Engineering
  • B.Tech – Naval Architecture & Ocean Engineering
  • B.Sc – Nautical Science
  • M.Tech – Marine Engineering
  • Maritime Law & Management programs

Admission Process

Admissions are based on the IMU-CET (Indian Maritime University – Common Entrance Test). Qualified candidates participate in centralized counselling for campus and program allocation.

Highlights

  • Central University with global maritime recognition
  • Strong placement links with shipping & logistics companies
  • Multiple campuses across India
  • Hands-on maritime and ship-training exposure
