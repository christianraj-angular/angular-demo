College Overview

Narsee Monjee Institute of Management Studies (NMIMS) is a leading deemed-to-be university in India. Engineering programs are offered through Mukesh Patel School of Technology Management & Engineering (MPSTME) across campuses like Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Shirpur, Bengaluru, Indore, and Hyderabad.

Popular Engineering Programs

  • B.Tech – Computer Science & Engineering
  • B.Tech – Artificial Intelligence
  • B.Tech – Data Science
  • B.Tech – Information Technology
  • B.Tech – Electronics Engineering

Admission Process

Admissions are primarily through NPAT (NMIMS Entrance Test), followed by counselling and document verification.

Highlights

  • Strong academic reputation & modern curriculum
  • Industry-integrated programs and labs
  • Good placement record in IT & consulting
  • Multiple campuses across India
