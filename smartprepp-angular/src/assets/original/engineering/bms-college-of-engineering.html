BMS College of Engineering (BMSCE)

One of India’s oldest and most reputed private engineering colleges.

Engineering College Autonomous Bengaluru

College Overview

BMS College of Engineering (BMSCE) was established in 1946 and is located in Bengaluru, Karnataka. It is an autonomous institution affiliated to VTU and is known for strong academics, discipline, and consistent placement performance.

Popular Engineering Programs

  • B.E – Computer Science & Engineering
  • B.E – Information Science & Engineering
  • B.E – Electronics & Communication Engineering
  • B.E – Mechanical Engineering
  • B.E – Civil Engineering
  • M.Tech & Research Programs

Admission Process

Admissions are through KCET and COMEDK. Limited seats are available through management quota based on eligibility.

Highlights

  • Autonomous status under VTU
  • Excellent campus placements
