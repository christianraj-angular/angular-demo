Jain University, Bangalore

A leading private university in Karnataka known for strong engineering, business and liberal studies programs.

Engineering College Karnataka Private University

College Overview

Jain University (Deemed-to-be University) is a well-known private institution based in Bengaluru. The university is recognised for its focus on industry-ready curriculum, entrepreneurship and research-driven learning. Its Faculty of Engineering & Technology offers a wide range of B.Tech and M.Tech programs.

Popular Engineering Programs

  • B.Tech – Computer Science & Engineering
  • B.Tech – Information Science & Engineering
  • B.Tech – Electronics & Communication Engineering
  • B.Tech – Mechanical Engineering
  • B.Tech – Aerospace Engineering
  • M.Tech specialisations in emerging technologies

Admission Process

B.Tech admissions are primarily done through Jain Entrance Test (JET) and also consider national-level exams where applicable. Short-listed candidates are invited for counselling and branch allocation based on merit and preference.

Highlights

  • NAAC A+ accredited university with strong academic reputation
  • Industry-oriented curriculum with project-based learning
  • Active incubation and entrepreneurship support
  • Good placement record with IT and core engineering companies
  • Vibrant campus life with clubs, sports and cultural activities
