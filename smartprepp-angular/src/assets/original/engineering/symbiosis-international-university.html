Engineering College Deemed University Pune

College Overview

Symbiosis International University is a leading private deemed university based in Pune, offering engineering programs through the Symbiosis Institute of Technology (SIT). The university is known for academic quality, industry exposure, and disciplined learning.

Popular Engineering Programs

  • B.Tech – Computer Science & Engineering
  • B.Tech – Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
  • B.Tech – Robotics & Automation
  • B.Tech – Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering
  • B.Tech – Mechanical Engineering

Admission Process

Admissions are through SITEEE or JEE Main, followed by counselling and branch allocation.
