Ramaiah Institute of Technology (MSRIT)

One of Bengaluru’s top private engineering colleges known for academics & placements.

Engineering College Bengaluru Autonomous

College Overview

Ramaiah Institute of Technology (MSRIT), established in 1962, is an autonomous engineering college located in Bengaluru, Karnataka. Affiliated to VTU, MSRIT is known for strong academics, research, and excellent placement outcomes.

Popular Engineering Programs

  • B.E – Computer Science & Engineering
  • B.E – Information Science
  • B.E – Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
  • B.E – Electronics & Communication Engineering
  • B.E – Mechanical & Civil Engineering
  • M.Tech & Research Programs

Admission Process

Admissions are primarily through KCET and COMEDK. Management quota seats are also available based on eligibility criteria.

Highlights

  • Autonomous institution under VTU
  • NAAC A+ Accredited
  • Excellent placements with top IT & core companies
  • Strong alumni & industry connections
